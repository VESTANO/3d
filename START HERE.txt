VICE COAST ’86 — OFFLINE GAME

1. Extract the ZIP into any folder.
2. Double-click Play Vice Coast.html. If asked, choose Edge, Chrome, or Firefox.
3. Choose Play story for the three jobs, or Free roam to explore the city.

WASD or arrow keys — walk, accelerate, reverse and steer
E — get in or out of a nearby parked car (slow down first)
Space — handbrake in a car; jump on foot
Shift — run on foot
Drag the view — look around
M — expand the map
Esc — pause

On mobile, use the on-screen arrows, CAR and BRAKE / JUMP buttons.
The diamond on the minimap marks your next destination. The timer starts
only after reaching the job’s starting marker in a car.

No installation, account, or internet connection is required. Progress
is saved in your browser when local storage is available. If 3D graphics
cannot start, the same city and jobs are playable from a top-down view.

This is an original Vice City–inspired browser game, not the Rockstar release.
